Do I need to pay to use Isle?

No. Isle is GPL v3 free software, and the source is public. Paying through Polar gives you the signed build and supports development.

Is there a license key?

No. There is no license key and no DRM. Support is about convenience, releases and sustainability.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Use the supporter build on your personal Macs. The public source remains available under GPL v3.

Why is Isle not on the Mac App Store?

Isle is GPL v3, and Apple's App Store terms conflict with GPL anti-DRM rules. Isle ships as a signed, notarized download instead.
