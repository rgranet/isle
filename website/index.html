Good to know.

Is Isle paid software?

The source is GPL v3. Supporting Isle pays for the signed, notarized build, updates and development.

Does Isle upload my code?

No. Isle talks to your agents locally on your Mac. There are no accounts and no telemetry.

Do I need a MacBook with a notch?

The notch gives the best experience. Isle can also render as a floating surface on other Macs.
